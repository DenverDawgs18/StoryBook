the studio

Turn your pages into a performance.

Paste a story or upload a manuscript. StoryBook finds the speakers and gives each one a voice. It chooses weather and music to suit the mood, and writes a sound effect wherever the prose names one. You get a mastered MP3.

The Studio needs the StoryBook server, which isn't reachable from this page. Run python -m storybook serve and open http://127.0.0.1:8000/studio.html.
