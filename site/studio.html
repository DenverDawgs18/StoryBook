The Studio

or, Your Pages, Performed

❦

Bring your own story, a book you've written or a tale you tell at bedtime, and hear it the way the library sounds: a voice for every character, weather and music made for the story, and a finished audiobook at the end.

the first

Bring your pages

Paste a story, or upload a manuscript. Chapters become chapters, and dialogue is found wherever someone speaks.

the second

Meet your cast

Every character gets a voice of their own. Hear each one, recast who you like, and choose the weather of the piece.

the third

Hear it performed

Rain where it rains, a knock where someone knocks, music under the quiet parts. A mastered audiobook you can keep.

The Studio needs the StoryBook server, which isn't reachable from this page. Run python -m storybook serve and open http://127.0.0.1:8000/studio.html.
